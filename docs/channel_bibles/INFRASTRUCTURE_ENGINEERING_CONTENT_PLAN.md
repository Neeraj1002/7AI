## 2. Audience and positioning

**Difference from GoDocs, in one sentence.** Same "how did they build that?" hook, but every video puts the real scale, cost and date on screen and shows the source, so the viewer who writes "you are greatly exaggerating" has nothing to argue with (E1, E4).

**Starting sub-niche: water, canals and rivers.** GoDocs' strongest first-page results sit there: Saudi water 6.7M, Suez 4.6M, China's rivers 648K, US desert water 612K (E1). Not every water topic works: its Aral Sea video shows 43K and the Corinth Canal 59K, so the hook matters more than the subject.

**Audience (inference, not measured).** Curious general viewers who watch explainers on history, maps and megaprojects, and who read comments to check the claims. Public data does not give their age or country, so the profile does not assume them.

**Language.** English assumed; change it in the settings table.

## 8. 90-day direction, risks and checks

The direction is to find the strongest pillar with the channel's own data by day 30, then spend days 31 to 90 on it. The founder sets the pass thresholds before day 1; this profile does not invent them.

1. **Days 1 to 30: test.** Publish the four weeks above, one pillar each. Record views, average view duration and impressions click-through rate for every video in YouTube Studio.
2. **Gate at day 30.** Rank the four pillars on those three numbers. Drop any pillar that is last on all three. Decide whether the Shorts spin-offs earned their place.
3. **Days 31 to 60: concentrate.** Make the top one or two pillars about 70% of output; keep one slot a week for a new idea from the lists.
4. **Gate at day 60.** Check whether the top pillar repeats its result on new topics, not just the first.
5. **Days 61 to 90: extend.** Build a recurring series inside the winning pillar, still varying structure.

**Risks**

| Risk | Why | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap with GoDocs | It owns the same hook (E1) | Skip topics it covered; lead with numbers and sources (E4) |
| Outlier-driven evidence | Its 6.7M video sits far above the rest (E1) | Test pillars equally before betting on one |
| Accuracy backlash | Viewers dispute claims in comments (E4) | Source check at the first human gate |
| Policy | AI label on the leading video (E5); one repeated template on another channel (E6) | Disclose; rotate structure |
| Shorts demand untested | No infrastructure Shorts seen (GAP) | Treat Shorts as a test in the first 30 days |
| Thin comment evidence | One video's comments read | Read comments on 3 to 4 more videos before day 30 |

**Check before any script uses it (all rows marked V):** the Colorado and Indus treaty details, the Panama history, the Opera House cost figures, the Big Dig cost and the 2006 collapse, Venice gate delays, the 1928 dam inspection, pyramid-lifting theories, Roman concrete findings, Angkor as a water city (scholars differ), and every Mexico City, Jakarta, airport-island and Tokyo figure.

## 3. Brief bank

App settings for every Cold Open Short: Type Short, Format Freeform, Length 45, Visuals AI images, Voice channel default, Music mysterious, Sources the Mary Celeste URLs, Max spend not needed. (From 30 Oct the Short Length is 40; see the spec, section 9.)

Design rules learned on S-1: 2 or 3 facts, part budgets that add up to about 110 words, a close of about 30 words (second-to-last at most 18 + last at most 12), and no more than one or two exact sentences.

## 4. Choosing cases (for the 9 open slots of the 19-video plan)

Score each candidate on five questions:
1. Is there a popular version that the record corrects?
2. Are the facts well sourced, with at least one primary source?
3. Is the sensitivity level 1 or 2?
4. Can it be told with objects and places, not faces? (Gate 2 bans recognisable real people and faces toward the camera.)
5. Does it have a date hook, such as an anniversary or the season?

Not for now: murders and violent true crime (level 3 or higher, face-dependent, possible ad limits, crowded). Since 30 Oct the focus is the safe crime types: old cons, non-violent heists and thefts, forgeries, and trials where everyone involved is long dead and the record is solid (section 7). Old art thefts also fit **The Trick**; ideas to research, unverified: the 1911 Mona Lisa theft, the 1990 Gardner Museum theft.

**Flex lane.** The weekly flex Short tries a different case type or era. Winners get a Long. One Long per type teaches little on a new channel; weekly Shorts give repeated samples.
